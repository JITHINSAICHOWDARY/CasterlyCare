CASTERLYCARE — PHASE 2.9.1
PATIENT MEDICINES ARCHITECTURE

1. PatientMedicines uses patientService.getMedicines only.
2. Active medicines are normalized safely before render.
3. Reminder times are sorted chronologically when parseable.
4. Missing reminder times are explicitly represented as not recorded.
5. Active prescription details show medicine, form, dosage, frequency, and reminder times.
6. Today's schedule explicitly avoids implying dose adherence/taken state.
7. Initial loading, empty, error, and retry states are present.
8. Responsive/mobile CSS is scoped to medicine surfaces.
9. Clinical medication disclaimer is present.
10. Backend /patient/medicines contract is unchanged.
