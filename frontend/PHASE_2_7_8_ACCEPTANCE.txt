CASTERLYCARE — PHASE 2.7.8 ACCEPTANCE

Patient appointment accessibility, mobile, and integration hardening
- Upcoming/Past tabs support Arrow Left/Right, Home, and End keyboard navigation.
- Only the active appointment tab is in the normal tab order.
- Appointment wizard step controls expose descriptive accessible labels.
- Reschedule validation errors are programmatically associated with date/time inputs.
- Appointment action buttons meet mobile touch-target sizing.
- Wizard step strip remains usable on narrow screens without page-level horizontal overflow.
- Reduced-motion behavior is preserved.
- Patient reschedule/cancel service methods remain wired to their explicit backend contracts.

Backend dependency note
- Patient-role reschedule/cancel routes are still not present in the available backend reference; frontend controls are intentionally non-speculative about server behavior.
