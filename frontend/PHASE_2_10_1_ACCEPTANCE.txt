CASTERLYCARE — PHASE 2.10.1
PATIENT ASSESSMENT & VITALS ARCHITECTURE

1. Assessment page uses the centralized patient service layer only.
2. Vitals form includes SpO2, heart rate, systolic BP, diastolic BP, and temperature.
3. Client-side validation rejects missing, non-numeric, and implausible values.
4. Blood-pressure cross-field validation requires systolic > diastolic.
5. Latest result uses professional monitoring language rather than diagnostic wording.
6. Longitudinal history is presented as monitoring trajectory data.
7. Assessment disclaimer explicitly states that automated monitoring is not diagnosis.
8. Existing backend POST /patient/assessment and GET /patient/assessment/history contracts are unchanged.
9. Loading, refresh, empty, validation-error, and submission-error states are present.
10. Accessibility semantics include labels, described-by errors, focus handling, and live status announcements.
11. Responsive mobile layout and reduced-motion behavior are included.
12. Real One-Class SVM and data-drift engine remain deferred to Phase 3 and are not falsely claimed as implemented here.
